The transcript preserves spoken wording and recognition errors. The overview maps blocks to slide pages and classroom emphasis. The notes organize concepts, derivations, examples and illustrations. Each serves a different purpose and remains available for comparison.

Student names, personal contact details and internal-only paths are removed or adapted in the public copy; the algorithm content and document structure are retained.
